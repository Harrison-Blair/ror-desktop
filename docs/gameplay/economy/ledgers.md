# Ledgers

Each player keeps a record for their character. A record can include:

- Cash or personal funds.
- Company accounts, kept as a separate account or section.
- Income and debts.
- Inventory and where it is.

All money is in dollars.

The player calculates each income from quantities and campaign prices. The game master objects only if the result is wrong.

[Sessions and turns](../sessions/turn-order.md#the-next-round) describes when income and debt payments settle. [Trade](trade.md) describes how players record a sale.

## Assets in several places

A character can keep different amounts of an asset in different locations. Ownership and location can differ. For example, a company can own a cargo while part of it is at sea and the rest is ashore. If the cargo at sea is lost, the company still has the portion ashore.

The [campaign map](../world/map.md) shows where characters built things and which places they are involved in.

Some players record where items go. Others remember the locations and keep only the purchase record.
