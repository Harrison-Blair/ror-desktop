# Trade

## Trades between players

The agreement of the players is enough. A trade needs no game master approval and no skill check.

The players set the terms. Payment can be immediate, on credit, in instalments, or on delivery.

Only the players record a trade. Each side records its own half in their [ledger](ledgers.md). The game master keeps no trade record.

### Example: a sale

One character sells 45 cameras to another for a total of $45,000.

The seller records the quantity sold and the total price. If the seller tracks the cameras in inventory, they subtract 45 cameras from that inventory. The seller adds $45,000 to the balance in their ledger. A description such as "Sold 45 cameras" explains the increase.

The buyer records the quantity purchased and the total price. The buyer deducts $45,000 from the account used for the purchase. The buyer now owns the 45 cameras and decides what to do with them.

## Trades with game master characters

The game master plays the other side and sets the price. The game master can ask for a [skill check](../sessions/skill-checks.md), for example to negotiate.
