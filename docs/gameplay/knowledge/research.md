# Research during play

A player in the current campaign plays a Russian oil baron. After the character bought land, the group paused to research three questions.

- How much oil did the land contain?
- What kind of oil did it contain?
- What did that oil cost at the time?

Other questions concern events in the campaign. For example, a player searched their notes to see whether they started a project and how far it progressed.

Anyone in the group can research during play. The group decides whether to pause the timer, and it usually pauses for longer research. Players also research between sessions.

## Campaign prices

The group can use a historical price as a starting point. After the group discusses it, the game master uses that price or adjusts it.

Some items have no historical price, for example a product that never existed. The group discusses a price, and the game master rules.
