# Group needs

This document records what slows play and what the group wants help with. It records needs. [The app index](../README.md) lists the features and the build order.

## Where play loses time

- **Note taking.** Players take notes during their own turn and during other players' turns. Notes take mental effort to write and to organize.
- **Research.** Players stop to work out realistic numbers. Each person knows only some areas, so research takes time. [Research](../../gameplay/knowledge/research.md) describes it.
- **Searching notes.** Players search their notes to find what they did and what is in progress.
- **Bookkeeping and calculations.** Players and the game master calculate balances, payments, and costs by hand. The group would hand most of this work to the app.
- **Returning after a gap.** The group does not play on a regular schedule. Sometimes months pass between sessions. After a gap, it is hard to see what was in progress and what is still outstanding, unless the player kept good notes.

## What the game master keeps track of

The game master currently holds a large amount in memory:

- World events.
- The state of each project and the calculations for it.
- Everything the game master did not write down. The game master reviews their notes and must remember the rest. The app can help only with what the notes hold.
- All game master characters: what they do, their ambitions, their stats, and where they enter or leave the story.

## What players look at during a turn

- Most often, their notes and their current open tasks.
- A to-do list of goals and future plans, for players who keep one.
- The character sheet. Today it is a graphic from the game master that shows the picture, the name, and the stats.
- The campaign map in Google Earth. [Campaign map](../../gameplay/world/map.md) describes it.

## Candidate areas

The group named these areas. [The build order](../README.md#build-order) sets their order.

- Notes that sync between players.
- Ledgers.
- The turn timer.
- Economy tracking.
