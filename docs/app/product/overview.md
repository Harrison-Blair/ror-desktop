# Ror Desktop

Ror Desktop helps a group manage a tabletop roleplaying campaign. The app has tools for campaign notes, people, sessions, and dice. The group can also use the chat agent.

## Purpose during play

The app should speed up play and help the players enjoy it. It should give players more time to act in the game. It should reduce the time players and the game master spend finding information, searching their notes, and making calculations.

The group would hand most of its bookkeeping to the app.

## How the group works today

Each player and the game master keep their own notes about actions and events. The documents vary in format. The group also uses online dice rollers and separate tools to time turns. Today, one player uses the app. The rest of the group does not use it yet.

[Group needs](needs.md) describes where play loses time today. The [feature inventory](feature-inventory.md) describes the current app.

## Economy tracking

The group wants to track the economy over time. The open question is how to do this with little bookkeeping for players. For example, players already research historical prices to understand economic conditions during play.
